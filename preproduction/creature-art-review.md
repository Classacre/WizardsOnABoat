# Creature concept-art review

16 September 2026. Visual inspection of the ten saved creature boards, compared with the supplied [Great Sea reference](references/great-sea-style-board.png) and the [creature proposals](research/creature-design.md). This is an internal review for presentation to the user, not user approval, a fidelity score, or a production-model sign-off.

All ten boards provide several useful views and retain a recognizable species across those views. I found no missing creature, unreadable sheet or severe generation defect that blocks meaningful review of the designs. The boards use the reference's cobalt/turquoise/ivory palette, broad painted facets, warm accents, sunny ocean vignettes and broken white foam. The creature rendering makes the faceting and color patches more explicit than the original environmental board; that is a visible stylistic choice for the user to assess.

## Review and handoff boundary

These sheets support choosing species, silhouettes, palette, temperament and major anatomical mechanisms now. Small differences in markings, foliage and pose do not need to be eliminated before that review. Scale inconsistencies and anatomy ambiguities should remain visible as notes rather than being silently treated as final measurements.

After the user selects designs, create a reconciled asset handoff with one agreed neutral pose, dimensions, limb/fin map, underside/back construction, material grouping and movement limits. A single-image reconstruction workflow should receive one isolated view, while the other approved views guide manual checking. None of these sheets proves mesh quality, rigging, collision, multiplayer behavior or laptop performance.
