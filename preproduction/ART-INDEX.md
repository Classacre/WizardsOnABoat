The collection covers a wizard and shopkeeper, a boat, starter island, modular buildings, eleven biomes across three boards, three dungeons, ten multi-angle creatures including two bosses, equipment, spell creation, physical spell effects, island paths, and a combined sailing scene.

The [creature-sheet review](creature-art-review.md) records observed anatomy, scale and modeling follow-ups. The pack is ready for concept feedback; user approval is still pending.

Board 05 uses the corrected v2 image: its shop caption now lists compasses, staffs and potions. The original v1 is retained as an unused generated draft, not part of the selected collection.
